02Insights

One signal is noise. Four is a pattern.

No single signal is alarming. Together, they're worth a call.

  3. Oct 15 · Two days later

  4. Oct 19 · Grace

04How it works

Built on Alexa+'s open standard.

Nova is a self-hosted MCP server, the open standard Alexa+ uses for third-party experiences. Each person in the circle links their own account with OAuth 2.1, and Amazon Nova on Amazon Bedrock does the reasoning.
