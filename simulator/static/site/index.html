06Safe and respectful

Careful by design.

P.01

Never diagnoses.

Nova notices and shows its evidence. Answers about medicine come only from the care team's own notes, with the source. Plan changes need a person's approval.

“Please don't take ibuprofen, Rose. Your knee discharge instructions say to avoid it because of blood pressure and kidney function.”

P.02

Asks before it shares.

When Rose mentions a symptom, she decides whether her family hears about it. Consent travels as an MCP elicitation.

“Would you like me to let Maya and Leo know you're feeling dizzy?”

P.03

Rose is in charge.

She chooses what the circle can see, one category at a time, and only she can change it. Identity comes from each person's token, never from what someone says.

“Okay. Your medications are now private from the circle.”

P.04

Emergencies skip the line.

Falls, chest pain, breathing trouble and stroke signs alert everyone at once and tell her to call 911. No question first.

“I've alerted Maya, Leo and Grace. If this is an emergency, please call 911.”
